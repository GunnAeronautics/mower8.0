Solid Green is Rocket Is In AIR

Blinking Green is Rocket Is READY

RGB Scrolling is Rocket Is Armed and READY

Solid Orange is pre-Startup / Servo testing

Blinking Blue is Rocket Is Arming (after pre Startup) / CONFIG Mode

Solid Red on left light means Fatal error, the right led will indicate error

Solid Blue on left light indicates Non Fatal error, the right led will indicate warning

Error/warning codes:
Right LED blinks white to indicate code

Long blinks = 10
Short blinks = 1


1 = bno errror
2 = adxl error
3 = baro error
4 = servo error
5 = sd error
